# FluxIQ Commercial Licensing

FluxIQ's community license is designed to keep personal, non-commercial, and
internal business use accessible while reserving uses that make FluxIQ part of
an offering to third parties.

## When A Separate Agreement Is Required

A separate written commercial agreement is required before you:

- provide customer-facing automation powered by FluxIQ;
- host or operate FluxIQ for customers or other third parties;
- offer FluxIQ as a managed or multi-tenant service;
- embed FluxIQ in a commercial product or service offered to third parties;
- redistribute, sublicense, repackage, or resell FluxIQ commercially; or
- white-label FluxIQ or remove its required notices or branding.

These rights may be offered through Business, Enterprise, Cloud, Embed, OEM,
white-label, marketplace, or other agreements. An ordinary internal-use
commercial agreement does not include hosting, redistribution, resale, OEM, or
white-label rights unless the signed agreement expressly says that it does.

## Inquiries

Contact [license@getfluxiq.com](mailto:license@getfluxiq.com) to discuss the
intended use and available terms. Pricing and scope are provided privately and
may depend on deployments, customers, usage, support, or distribution rights.

An inquiry, negotiation, quote, or invoice does not itself grant commercial
rights. Those rights begin only when the licensor and customer execute a
written agreement covering the intended use.
