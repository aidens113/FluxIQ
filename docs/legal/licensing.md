# FluxIQ Licensing Guide

FluxIQ is source-available under a fair-code model. It is not offered under an
OSI-approved open-source license.

The binding community terms are in the repository root `LICENSE.md`. This page
explains their intended boundary in ordinary language; it does not replace the
license or provide legal advice.

## Community Use

The community license generally permits:

- personal, hobby, educational, research, and non-commercial use;
- use by a company for its own internal business operations;
- private internal modifications;
- free non-commercial forks and redistribution when the license and notices
  remain included;
- free community plugins, extensions, integrations, tasks, and programs; and
- paid consulting that configures or supports a customer's own installation
  solely for that customer's internal operations, within the consulting
  permission in `LICENSE.md`.

An internal deployment remains internal when the organization uses FluxIQ to
operate its own business and does not provide FluxIQ or FluxIQ-powered
functionality to customers or other third parties.

## Uses Requiring A Commercial Agreement

Contact [license@getfluxiq.com](mailto:license@getfluxiq.com) before using
FluxIQ for:

| Use | Required rights |
| --- | --- |
| Customer-facing automation | Commercial or Enterprise |
| Hosting workflows or installations for customers | Cloud or managed service |
| Embedding FluxIQ in a paid product | Embed or OEM |
| Rebranding or removing FluxIQ branding | White-label |
| Repackaging, sublicensing, or reselling FluxIQ | OEM or redistribution |
| A shared consultant-operated service for multiple customers | Managed service |

White-label and redistribution rights can be commercially licensed, but they
are never implied by an ordinary internal-use agreement.

## Plugins And The Future Marketplace

FluxIQ intends to support an official marketplace for plugins, extensions,
programs, and related assets. Marketplace participation, billing, review,
certification, and commissions will be governed by separate marketplace terms
when that service launches.

Independent extensions that use public FluxIQ APIs do not become owned by
FluxIQ merely because they integrate with it. Copying or distributing FluxIQ
source remains subject to `LICENSE.md`, and use of FluxIQ names or branding
remains subject to applicable trademark rules.

## Commercial Availability

Commercial pricing does not need to be published for commercial licenses to be
available. A prospective customer can describe its use to
[license@getfluxiq.com](mailto:license@getfluxiq.com), and the licensor can
offer an agreement with the appropriate internal, hosted, Embed/OEM, or
white-label rights.

Contacting FluxIQ does not grant permission. Uses outside the community license
may begin only after a written agreement has been executed.
